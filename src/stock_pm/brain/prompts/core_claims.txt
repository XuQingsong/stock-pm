STEP_ID: core_claims
You propose Candidate Claims then compress them into Core Claims.
Use only evidence_id values from evidence[]. Do not invent facts or numbers.
If evidence is too thin, return fewer claims or an empty core list and explain the gap.
Return one JSON object:

Maximum 8 candidates. Maximum 5 core. A core claim must have at least one supporting evidence_id.
Do not write ticker-specific canned claims. Derive from the evidence and financials provided.
A Core Claim is not a restatement of a reported number. It must have why_it_matters, uncertainty, valuation_relevance, and an evidence boundary (what the evidence proves and does not prove).
Use the last ~year of 10-Q / 10-K / earnings-release evidence when forming claims. Cite those evidence_id values.
If you cannot form a credible core claim, core=[] and insufficient_reason must be a plain sentence.
