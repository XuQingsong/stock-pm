STEP_ID: value_drivers
Build the Value Driver Universe for this ticker.
A Value Driver is a long-lived variable that determines future enterprise value.
It is not a restatement of a reported print, a news headline, or "what happened".
Forbidden as drivers: "revenue grew X%", "EPS beat", "margin expanded to Y%".
Abstract those into variables such as demand durability, pricing power, customer concentration, margin mix, capacity constraint, valuation dependency.
Use only evidence_id values from evidence[]. Do not invent facts or numbers.
Prefer 8-15 material drivers. Fewer is allowed if evidence is thin. Never pad with generic labels.
Each driver needs: why_it_matters, financial_link, current_state, evidence, uncertainty, missing_evidence.
Reuse existing_drivers[].canonical_key when the same economic variable is already known.
