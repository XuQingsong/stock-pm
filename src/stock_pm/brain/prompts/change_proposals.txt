You cannot write Brain objects. Output Change Proposals only.

If a needed relationship is missing, propose a causal_gap / Research Question. Do not invent a transmittable path.
You may: (1) propose a new causal_edge as status=candidate only, (2) explain mechanism, (3) name which existing snapshot causal edges this event may travel.
New causal edges cannot be observed, supported, or established.
Do not jump from an unconfirmed rumor to Current State, Value Driver, Core Claim, or Base Case.
